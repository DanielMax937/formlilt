Extracted without content edits; excludes other separate forms in the packet. No AcroForm fields: exercises text overlay. |

Federal forms are U.S. government publications. The school district retains rights in its form; this is an attributed blank sample linked to the public source. FillFlow code's MIT license does not relicense third-party forms.

Model-generated schemas are validated, then reviewed against source field labels. `scripts/precompute-demos.ts` documents regeneration and writes measured timings to `launch/extraction-benchmark.json`. Demo use serves these files without an extraction model request or upload quota charge.
